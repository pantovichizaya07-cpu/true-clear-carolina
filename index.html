Questions, answered

Do I need to be home?

For exterior-only cleans, no — as long as we can reach an outdoor water spigot. For inside cleans, yes, someone needs to be home.

What if it rains?

We reschedule free of charge. Rain on freshly cleaned windows doesn't dirty them — but we'd rather work in the dry, and so would you.

How do I pay?

Cash, Cash App, or Zelle. A small deposit locks your appointment; the rest is due when the job is done and you're happy with it.

Do you use ladders?

No. We work single-story homes and pole-reachable second-story windows. If a window genuinely needs a ladder, we'll tell you honestly before booking.

Where do you work?

Lexington, Thomasville, and Burlington, NC, and nearby towns. If you're close by, ask — we'll probably say yes.

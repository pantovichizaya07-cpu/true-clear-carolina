    Questions

    Straight answers.

    Something else? Add it to the notes when you book and we'll answer when we confirm.

    Do I need to be home?

    For exterior-only cleans, no — as long as we can reach an outdoor water spigot and the gate is open. For inside cleans, someone needs to be home.

    How fast will my booking be confirmed?

    Usually within a few hours during the day. Your tracking page updates the moment we confirm, and we'll text you too.

    What if it rains?

    We reschedule free of charge if it's pouring. And if rain spoils a fresh clean within 48 hours, we come back and redo it free.

    How do I pay?

    Cash, Cash App, or Zelle. We may ask for a small deposit to lock in your slot once it's confirmed; the rest is due when the job's done and you're happy with it.

    Do you use ladders?

    No. We clean single-story homes and pole-reachable second-story windows. If a window truly needs a ladder, we'll say so honestly before we confirm.

    Where do you work?

    Lexington, Thomasville, Burlington and nearby towns. If you're close by, book anyway — we'll let you know if you're out of range.

    Can I cancel or reschedule?

    Yes. Your tracking page has a cancel button, and you can rebook a new time in two minutes. No fees.
